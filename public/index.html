Rules

  1. Each player places 3 stones on empty nodes.
  2. After all 6 stones are placed, players move one stone per turn.
  3. A stone may move only to a connected empty node.
  4. The first player to make 3 stones in a straight line wins.
  5. The center node connects to every other node.

Tutorial

Learn the most important Three Stones strategies.

  1. Control the center: it connects to every node.
  2. Watch for two in a row: block the third spot.
  3. Make threats: set up a line where you can win next turn.
